Questions

Before you install.

Is AutoVerse really free?

Yes. The map uses free OpenStreetMap data and the routing runs on the head unit itself, so there are no API keys, accounts or subscriptions to pay for.

Which head units does it work on?

Any Android head unit or tablet running Android 6.0 or newer, including common 9 and 10 inch units with 2 GB of RAM. It adapts to 1024×600, 1280×720 and wide screens.

Does it need internet?

The map tiles, place search and weather need internet, which most drivers share from their phone's hotspot. Routing runs on the unit: the first route in a new area downloads that area's road data once (about 20 to 60 MB).

Can I still use Google Maps?

Yes. Find any place in Google Maps and tap Share → AutoVerse, and the same route opens on AutoVerse's map. You can also open Google Maps from AutoVerse at any time.

Does it work with iPhone?

Yes. Phone music over Bluetooth and the QR phone remote both work with iPhone and Android, with nothing to install on the phone.

What does AutoVerse do with my location?

Your location stays on the unit. Only a rounded position (about 1 km) is sent for weather, and place searches send what you type. There is no account, tracking or advertising.
